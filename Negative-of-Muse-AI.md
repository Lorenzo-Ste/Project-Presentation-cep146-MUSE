# Negative Effects of Meta Muse AI on People

## 1. Privacy Risks

Meta Muse AI may access personal information through connected applications, such as emails and personal address. This creates privacy concerns because sensitive information could be exposed or misused.

## 2. AI Errors and Trust

AI agents are not always accurate. Muse AI may misunderstand instructions or make mistakes when completing tasks. Users should check important actions before trusting the results.

## 3. Security Risks

AI agents may face cybersecurity threats, such as prompt injection attacks. Hackers could use malicious instructions to influence an AI agent's actions. This may put users' information and security at risk.

## Conclusion

Although Meta Muse AI can make people's lives easier, it may also create privacy, security, and reliability concerns. People should use AI carefully and review important actions.

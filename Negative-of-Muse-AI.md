# Negative Effects of Meta Muse AI on People

## 1. Privacy Risks

Meta Muse AI can access personal information through connected applications. According to TIME (2026), Muse creates detailed profiles based on information about users and their relationships.

This raises concerns about how personal data is collected, stored, and controlled.

**Source:** [TIME — Meta's Muse AI Agent Is Building a Dossier On You](https://ca.time.com/meta-muse-ai-agent-privacy/)

## 2. AI Errors and Unexpected Actions

AI agents can make mistakes when performing tasks for users. Fast Company (2026) reported an incident in which a user claimed that Muse shared his home address during a Facebook Marketplace transaction without his explicit approval.

This example shows why people should review important actions performed by AI agents.

**Source:** [Fast Company — Meta's Muse Is Taking Off. So Are the Privacy Concerns](https://www.fastcompany.com/91620148/metas-muse-is-taking-off-so-are-the-privacy-concerns)

## 3. Security Risks

AI agents can face cybersecurity threats, including prompt injection attacks. NIST (2025) explains that attackers may place malicious instructions in websites, emails, or files to influence an AI agent's behavior.

Meta also acknowledges that Muse may make mistakes and has introduced security protections to reduce these risks.

**Sources:**

- [NIST — Strengthening AI Agent Hijacking Evaluations](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2025/01/technical-blog-strengthening-ai-agent-hijacking-evaluations)
- [Meta — How We Built Safety Into Muse](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse)

## Conclusion

Meta Muse AI has the potential to make everyday tasks easier, but it also raises concerns about privacy, security, and reliability. Users should understand its permissions and review important actions instead of trusting AI completely.
